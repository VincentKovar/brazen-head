Brazen Head recordings
======================
Drop one audio file per fortune in this folder. mp3 is best (mono, 32-48 kbps keeps phones fast).
The names below are the ones the page looks for; fortunes without a file are spoken by the browser voice.
To add a fortune: add { text: "...", audio: "fortune-13.mp3" } to the FORTUNES list in the HTML,
then put fortune-13.mp3 here.

fortune-01.mp3
    Thou shalt not perish until thou hast sung mass at Jerusalem. Mark well, traveler, which Jerusalem is meant; there are more than one, and the Head is a poor liar.

fortune-02.mp3
    I was schooled among the Saracens, who count the stars better than kings count coin. The stars say yea, but not before the third moon wanes.

fortune-03.mp3
    Time is. Time was. Time is past. Seize thy hour, pilgrim, ere the brass grows cold.

fortune-04.mp3
    Seven years was I forged and seven more I slept. Patience: that which thou seekest keeps its own long hours.

fortune-05.mp3
    A wall of brass shall rise about thy house, yet it must be built of deeds and not of hope. Lay the first stone on the morrow.

fortune-06.mp3
    The Knights of the Temple kept a head such as I, and kept their counsel also. Do likewise; a secret told is a coin spent.

fortune-07.mp3
    The ichor rises in my vessel, the cams turn true, and the answer is yea. Yet beware the one who smiles and holds the key.

fortune-08.mp3
    Mercury hangs low in the house of the Scales. Settle thy debts, forgive a rival, and the road shall open before thee.

fortune-09.mp3
    Trust not the watchman who sleeps at his post. The hour of thy truth passes while he dreams, and brass, once broken, speaks no more.

fortune-10.mp3
    A fountain sings in a garden thou hast not yet seen. Thither wilt thou go before the year is out, and be glad of it.

fortune-11.mp3
    Not all that is spoken by a head of brass is true, nor all that is spoken by a head of flesh. Weigh both upon the balance of thine own heart.

fortune-12.mp3
    The astrolabe turns and the rete aligns: what thou fearest is smaller than its shadow. Go forth.
